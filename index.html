Our mission

Hind Holistics is not just another medical institute. We are building the *medical *army *of *India, a force of healers, researchers and innovators who will make Indian healthcare the most trusted in the world.

Hind Holistics Private Limited is a long-term commitment to redesign how a nation of more than a billion people is cared for. The future of Indian healthcare will be decided by people who act now.

We bring clinicians, scientists and technologists into one team with one purpose: research that reaches the bedside, AI that serves doctors and patients, and care that treats the whole human being.

About Hind Holistics

An Indian healthcare and wellness company

Hind Holistics Private Limited is an Indian healthcare and wellness company building accessible healthcare solutions through digital health, preventive healthcare and nutrition. Founded by general surgeon Dr. Deep Satiar, Hind Holistics brings doctors, researchers and technologists together to make good care easier to reach for people across India.

Accessible healthcare

Care that is easier to reach, understand and afford, wherever people live in India.

Digital health

Technology and AI that support doctors and patients, built around real clinical needs.

Preventive healthcare

Helping people stay well through early awareness, screening and healthy habits.

Nutrition & wellness

Everyday nutrition and lifestyle guidance that treats the whole person.

FOUNDER
Founder

Dr. Deep Satiar

General Surgeon · Founder, Hind Holistics Pvt. Ltd.

Dr. Deep Satiar is a general surgeon with vast experience in the surgical field, a part-time content creator, and the founder of Hind Holistics. Dr. Satiar started Hind Holistics with one conviction: India's healthcare future will be decided by the people who act now.

Beyond the operating theatre, Dr. Satiar has founded multiple NGOs and worked with many others, bringing care to the communities that need it most. Every part of that journey, from surgery to social work to the startup, serves one aim: to build a medical army that makes Indian healthcare the most trusted in the world.
