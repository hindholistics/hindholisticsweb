Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Hind Holistics Private Limited ("Hind Holistics", "we", "us") handles personal data shared through www.hindholistics.com.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

1. Who we are

Hind Holistics Private Limited is a company incorporated in India (CIN: U86100WB2025PTC285474). For any privacy question or request, contact us at hind.holistics@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you use the contact form on this website, we collect your name, email address, the reason for contacting us, your field or profession, and your message. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number or email address.

Please do not send medical records or sensitive health information through the website form, WhatsApp or email. This website does not provide medical consultations.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data needed to deliver and secure the site, such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages requested.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. Embedded third-party services, where present, follow their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

3. How we use your information

  • To read and reply to your message or application.
  • To discuss joining the team, partnerships or collaborations you have asked about.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers that process data on our behalf, only as needed to run this website and respond to you:

  • Netlify (website hosting and form submissions).
  • Google (email, through Gmail, and web fonts).
  • Meta / WhatsApp, if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.

Some of these providers may store or process data outside India. We may also disclose information if required by law or by a lawful request from authorities.

5. How long we keep it

We keep messages and contact details only for as long as needed to respond to you and for any follow-up you have agreed to, and then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you may:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, and withdraw any consent you have given;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us, and, if it is not resolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email hind.holistics@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

The website is served only over secure HTTPS connections. We take reasonable steps to protect personal data, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

8. Children

This website is not directed at children. If you are under 18, please contact us only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
