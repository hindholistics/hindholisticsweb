About us

About Hind Holistics

Hind Holistics Private Limited is an Indian healthcare and wellness company building accessible healthcare solutions through digital health, preventive healthcare and nutrition.

Who we are

Hind Holistics was founded by general surgeon Dr. Deep Satiar with a long-term goal: to help build the future of Indian healthcare. We are bringing together doctors, researchers and technologists into one team, which we call the medical army of India, so that good care becomes easier to reach for people across the country.

"Holistic" is in our name for a reason. We look at health as a whole: preventing illness, caring for the body and mind, and supporting people with the right information and tools, not only treating disease once it appears.

What we focus on

Our work is organised around four areas.

Accessible healthcare

Care that is easier to reach, understand and afford, wherever people live in India.

Digital health

Technology and AI that support doctors and patients, built around real clinical needs.

Preventive healthcare

Helping people stay well through early awareness, screening and healthy habits.

Nutrition & wellness

Everyday nutrition and lifestyle guidance that treats the whole person.

How we work

  • Evidence-based. We build on established medical evidence and clinical guidelines, and we are clear about what is and isn't proven.
  • Clinician-led. Our work is shaped by practising doctors who see real patients and real problems.
  • Built for India. We design for Indian patients, Indian health systems and Indian realities first.
  • Long-term. We are building carefully, for the next decades, not for quick headlines.

Our vision

We want Indian healthcare to be among the most trusted in the world. Our long-term pillars are research and development, AI in healthcare, better quality of life, and next-generation models of care and medical education.

Our founder

Dr. Deep Satiar

General Surgeon · Founder, Hind Holistics Private Limited

Dr. Deep Satiar is a general surgeon with vast experience in the surgical field and a part-time content creator. Beyond the operating theatre, Dr. Satiar has founded multiple NGOs and worked with many others, bringing care to the communities that need it most.

Hind Holistics brings that experience together with one aim: to build a medical army that makes Indian healthcare the most trusted in the world.
