Our mission

Hindholistics is not just another medical institute. We are building the *medical *army *of *India, a force of healers, researchers and innovators who will make Indian healthcare the most trusted in the world.

Hindholistics Private Limited is a long-term commitment to redesign how a nation of more than a billion people is cared for. The future of Indian healthcare will be decided by people who act now.

We bring clinicians, scientists and technologists into one team with one purpose: research that reaches the bedside, AI that serves doctors and patients, and care that treats the whole human being.

Founder

Dr. Deep Satiar

General Surgeon · Founder, Hindholistics Pvt. Ltd.

Dr. Deep Satiar is a general surgeon with vast experience in the surgical field, a part-time content creator, and the founder of Hindholistics. Dr. Satiar started Hindholistics with one conviction: India's healthcare future will be decided by the people who act now.

Beyond the operating theatre, Dr. Satiar has founded multiple NGOs and worked with many others, bringing care to the communities that need it most. Every part of that journey, from surgery to social work to the startup, serves one aim: to build a medical army that makes Indian healthcare the most trusted in the world.
